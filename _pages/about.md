I am a final-year PhD candidate in Engineering at the [University of Warwick](https://warwick.ac.uk/) (WMG, Intelligent Vehicle Sensors Group), supervised by Prof. Valentina Donzella, Prof. Kurt Debattista, and Prof. Mehrdad Dianati, and fully funded by the [CDT in Future Mobility Technologies](https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/wmg/education/cdt/fmt). I am currently a Visiting Researcher at [ETH Zurich](https://prs.igp.ethz.ch/), hosted by Prof. Konrad Schindler and Dr. Christos Sakaridis at the Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing Group.

My research focuses on **multi-modal sensor perception for autonomous driving** — in particular, 3D scene reconstruction, generative AI for driving data synthesis, controllable image/video generation, adverse-weather perception, and trustworthy AI for safety-critical vision systems.

Research Interests
======
- 3D scene reconstruction (neural fields, Gaussian splatting, depth completion)
- Generative AI for autonomous driving and robotics (Sim2Real, controllable video generation)
- Adverse-weather perception (fog, low-light, rain)
- Multi-modal scene understanding (camera &ndash; LiDAR fusion)
- Trustworthy AI for safety-critical vision systems
